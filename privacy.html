Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Shorts Uploader ("the tool") handles data. The tool is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data the tool accesses

  • YouTube account authorisation: an OAuth access token for the owner's channels, with permission to upload videos and to read and manage the channels' own videos and comments.
  • Channel data: titles, descriptions, privacy status and public statistics (views, likes, comment counts) of videos the tool uploaded, and the text of public comments on those videos.

How the data is used

  • To upload the owner's videos and set their titles, descriptions and publish times.
  • To show the owner how each video performs, so future videos can be improved.
  • To read comments on the owner's videos, find questions viewers ask, and draft replies for the owner.

The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone. It is not used to build profiles of commenters or other users.

Storage and security

  • Authorisation tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private repository and are never published.
  • Video statistics and summaries are kept in that private repository. Comment text is processed to find topic ideas; individual commenters' names are not stored.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's Third-party access page. Data received from Google APIs is deleted on request or when access is revoked.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
