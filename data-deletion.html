Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 24 November 2026

Shorts Uploader is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts. It does not collect data about other people.

How to remove access and delete data

  1. Facebook / Instagram: open Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Shorts Uploader and click Remove. On Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Remove.
  2. YouTube / Google: remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  3. After access is removed, all stored data received from these platforms (access tokens and video statistics) is deleted within 7 days.
  4. To request deletion directly, open an issue on GitHub; it is completed within 7 days.

See also the Privacy Policy.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
